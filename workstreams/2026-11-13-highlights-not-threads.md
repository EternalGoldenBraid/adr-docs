# Highlights without resolve state

Requester: Nicklas (via the mokki agent, 13 Nov).

Bare highlights (no question) and notes currently appear as unresolved
threads in the history, so they clutter triage. The user wants:

1. **Separate kind:** highlights and notes are listed apart from threads (own
   tab or filter) and have no resolved/unresolved state.
2. **Delete comment, keep highlight:** remove the comment text from a
   highlight after the fact, leaving the highlight on the page.
3. **Delete highlight:** remove the highlight itself.

Related: the earlier "Note mode" proposal (quiet notes that don't reach the
agent). Likely the same mechanism.
